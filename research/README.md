# Research Diary

This is the sleeves-up layer: where investigations happen in public, walls
and all. The wiki upstairs keeps only conclusions; this layer keeps the
process — the dead ends, the wrong turns, and the calls we were embarrassed
by later.

## The loop

ingest → questions → research → answers (with confidence) → new questions.

Questions are born two ways: from ingests (a source raises something we
can't yet answer) and from research itself (answering one question exposes
two more). Both land on the [question board](questions.md).

## How an investigation works

1. It starts from a board entry with an owner-signed **why it matters**.
2. It gets its own folder: `research/YYYY-MM-DD-slug/` — created when the
   work starts, named by start date. (No folder exists until a file is in
   it; that's fine.)
3. Inside, we write as we go: what we tried, what we assumed, where we hit
   a wall. Entries are dated. Wrong intermediate conclusions stay in —
   struck through or annotated, never deleted.
4. When it closes, two things happen:
   - The **conclusion** is filed upstairs in the wiki, with a confidence
     level and links back down to this folder.
   - New questions raised by the finding go onto the board.
5. The board and the wiki `log.md` record the closure.

## Honesty rules

Same house rules as the wiki, in plain words: **we never delete, we
correct.** Confidence downgrades get written down, not quietly edited out.
If an investigation dies on the vine, its board entry moves to *Abandoned*
with a one-line reason — abandoned questions are data too.
