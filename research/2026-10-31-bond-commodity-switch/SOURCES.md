# Sources — bond ↔ commodity switch

Tiered reading list. Status per source: unread / read / wiki-filed.
Nothing here is wiki-filed from this folder yet.

## ★ Shortlist — read even if nothing else

- **QUANTT (El-Ayari, Jarvis, Farivar, Simone, Zachari), "Macro
  Regime–Based Asset Rotation," Feb 2026** — *why:* closest published
  precedent found (2026-10-31 web pass): a full, honest methodology for
  testing regime-switching asset rotation (walk-forward, bootstrap
  significance, cost sensitivity). We steal standards, not results —
  they use macro indicators, we use a price ratio.
  <https://quantt.ca/papers/Macro%20Regime.pdf> — status: unread
- **AQR working paper (NBER w22793), "Commodities for the Long Run"** —
  *why:* defines the basket, its state table (14.8% / −4.7% inflation
  split), its construction limits, and its own investability caveat.
  <https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w22793/w22793.pdf>
  — status: wiki-filed (regimes/commodities-for-the-long-run.md)
- **Portfolio Optimizer, "Charles Gave's Four Quadrants Framework"** —
  *why:* the ancestor signal (Currency Debasement Ratio, 7-year
  average, threshold 1) in its exact published form; our variant
  deviates from it, so the deviation must be deliberate.
  <https://portfoliooptimizer.io/macro-indicators/four-quadrants/> —
  status: wiki-filed (regimes/growth-inflation-quadrants.md)

## Mechanism / context tier

- **Man Group, "The Road Ahead: Regime-Based Investing" (May 2026
  edition)** — *why:* the skeptic's rulebook — which regime signals
  persisted ("ALWAYS" findings) and the "everything is messy" warning
  that H2 must survive.
  <https://www.man.com/insights/road-ahead-regime-based-investing> —
  status: wiki-filed
- **Bridgewater, "The All Weather Story"** — *why:* the case *against*
  switching (hold all quadrants; surprises move prices, not levels).
  The null hypothesis of this investigation in essay form.
  <https://www.bridgewater.com/resources/all-weather-story.pdf> —
  status: wiki-filed
- **The Capital Process, "Gold vs Bonds" (Mar 2026)** — *why:*
  background only; a plain statement of the bond/gold see-saw we are
  replacing with a bond/commodity see-saw. Blog-grade; do not cite
  numbers from it.
  <https://thecapitalprocess.com/gold-vs-bonds-how-they-behave-in-different-economic-environments/>
  — status: unread, low tier
